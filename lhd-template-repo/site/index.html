Appendix

Method and definitions

How answers were collected

  • 7 questions × 8 cities, asked on 6 October 2026 (AEDT) through DataForSEO's AI Optimization and SERP APIs.
  • ChatGPT ran with web search from Australia. Gemini and Google AI Mode were located in each city. Perplexity (Sonar) and Claude (Sonnet 4.6) ran with web search, Australian results.
  • Each question was asked once per assistant. AI answers vary between runs, so treat single-cell results as indicative and city totals as the signal.
  • Google results: the same 7 searches in keyword form from each city, desktop, top 20 results plus map pack and AI Overview.

How answers were scored

  • Every law firm named in an answer was extracted, including map cards shown with the answer. Directories, regulators and insurers were excluded.
  • Name variants were merged (for example “Law Partners Injury Lawyers” into Law Partners). A firm counts once per answer.
  • Each LHD mention was checked against the answer text. “LHD” meaning a NSW Local Health District was not counted.
  • Trend data: DataForSEO LLM Mentions database, Google AI answers in Australia, Aug 2025 to Sep 2026.
