Appendix

Method and definitions

How answers were collected

  • 7 commercial questions × 8 Australian markets, evaluated in October 2026 across 5 verified AI answer engines (ChatGPT Search, Google AI Mode, Gemini 3.8, Perplexity Sonar, Claude Sonnet 4.6).
  • Google AI Mode and Gemini evaluated from each city market; ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude evaluated with Australian commercial search context.
  • Each question was asked across the multi-platform evaluation matrix to establish market standings.
  • Google results: matching commercial searches in each city recording Google Map Packs and organic top 20.

How answers were scored

  • Every performance marketing agency named in an answer was extracted in order of appearance.
