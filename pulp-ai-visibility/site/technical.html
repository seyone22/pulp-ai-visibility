5.0 Empirical Metric 1

Share of Model Voice (SoMV) & Wilson Score 95% CI

Formal Mathematical Definition

Unweighted probability of entity retrieval across observation set $N$:

$$\widehat{\text{SoMV}}(E) = \left( \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbb{I}(E \in \text{Entities}(R_i)) \right) \times 100\%$$

Wilson Score Interval with Continuity Correction

Standard asymptotic normal intervals fail near boundaries ($\hat{p} \to 0$). We mandate the Wilson Score Interval with continuity correction:

$$w = \frac{2n\hat{p} + z^2 \pm \left( z \sqrt{z^2 - \frac{1}{n} + 4n\hat{p}(1-\hat{p}) + (4\hat{p} - 2)} + 1 \right)}{2(n + z^2)}$$

6.0 Empirical Metric 2

Weighted Visibility Index (WVI) & DCG Logarithmic Decay

Discounted Cumulative Gain (DCG) Formulation

Being recommended #1 carries vastly more commercial conversion weight than being listed 5th. We formulate WVI with logarithmic position decay and stance polarity $S_{ik}$:

$$\text{WVI}(E) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( \sum_{k=1}^{K_i} \frac{S_{ik}}{\log_2(r_{ik} + 1)} \cdot \mathbb{I}(E = \text{Entity}_{ik}) \right)$$

Stance Polarity Multipliers ($S_{ik}$)

  • +1.0 (Strongly Recommended): Primary recommendation, category superlatives.
  • +0.5 (Neutral / Listed): Unranked enumeration, directory bullet point.
  • -0.5 (Cautionary / Negative): Explicit mention of high fees, complaints, or drawbacks.
  • 0.0 (Unranked): Brand completely omitted from answer.

12.0 Econometric Causal Modeling

Multi-Variable Logistic Regression Formulation

Econometric Problem Formulation

To establish statistical causality between traditional SERP signals and generative engine recommendation, we specify a logit regression predicting the probability of AI citation $P(Y_i = 1)$:

$$\ln\left(\frac{P(Y_i=1)}{1 - P(Y_i=1)}\right) = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X_{\text{MapPack}} + \beta_2 \ln(X_{\text{Reviews}} + 1) + \beta_3 X_{\text{Rating}} + \beta_4 X_{\text{DirCentrality}} + \beta_5 X_{\text{OrgRank}} + \epsilon_i$$

Identification Strategy

We exploit geographic cross-sectional variance across 8 discrete markets to isolate the causal effect of physical Local 3-Pack presence vs domain-level organic authority.

Covariate Operationalization

  • $X_{\text{MapPack}}$: Binary indicator ($1 = \text{Ranked } \le 3 \text{ in Google Local Map 3-Pack}, 0 = \text{Otherwise}$).
  • $\ln(X_{\text{Reviews}} + 1)$: Natural log of verified Google Business Profile review count (handles skewness).
  • $X_{\text{Rating}}$: Google star rating (continuous, $1.0$ to $5.0$).
  • $X_{\text{DirCentrality}}$: Eigenvector centrality across top directories (Clutch, Shopify Plus, Semrush).
  • $X_{\text{OrgRank}}$: Desktop organic Google rank ($1$ to $20$; $21$ if unranked).
