## Frequently asked questions

How does Pulp Digital measure success?

Profit, not vanity metrics. We track CAC and incremental revenue against your baseline, then report on it in a cadence you actually understand. Every campaign ties back to one question: is this making you money.

How is Pulp different from other agencies?

Most agencies split account managers and media buyers into separate teams that barely talk, let alone have a creative strategist working alongside them. We don't do this. Our team works collaboratively across all areas of your strategy, creative and media buying as a single system. It's how we've taken brands from $20K to $500K in monthly Meta spend in under six months.

What brands do you work with?

We partner with ambitious 7–9 figure eCommerce brands across lifestyle, beauty, fashion and FMCG, as well as other consumer-focused industries. Our sweet spot is working with brands that are ready to scale and treat creative and paid media as growth levers, not just expenses.

Who is working on my account?

Our team of specialists support you from day one, backed by our creative and paid media team. Our performance managers are very experienced, with no juniors leading anything. You'll always know exactly who's across your brand and what they're doing with it.

How long before we see results?

We run every account to a 30, 60, 90 day roadmap. Month one is volume testing — multiple hooks, angles and personas. Month two we double down on what's working and consolidate the budget. Month three we scale the proven combinations while a test pool keeps running underneath, so you're never riding a single winner.
